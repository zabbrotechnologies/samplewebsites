06 STUDIO PHILOSOPHY

NOT HERE TO FIT IN.

OFFGRID exists to explore the space between structure and spontaneity, precision and chaos, function and expression.

The world has enough perfectly neutral sans-serif interfaces designed by committee. We are dedicated to the tactile energy of printed matter, the joyful collision of unexpected weights, and the poetry of visual disruption.
