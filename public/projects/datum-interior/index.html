Disciplines

Most clients come to us for one thing and stay for the rest. Architecture, interiors, renovation and the objects inside are drawn by the same small team.

Architecture

01

New houses, extensions and small buildings, from the first site visit to the last inspection.

Feasibility, concept, permits, site supervision

Interiors

02

Complete interiors for homes, hotels and restaurants, down to the light fittings and fabric.

Space planning, joinery, lighting, styling

Renovation

03

Surveys, heritage advice, structure, restoration

How we work

Five steps from the first visit to the day you move in.

01

1 to 2 weeks

First visit

We walk the site or the rooms with you, listen, measure and photograph. You leave with a short brief written in your own words.

02

3 to 6 weeks

Concept

Sketches, simple card models and a first idea of cost, so we agree on direction before anything becomes expensive.

03

2 to 4 months

Design and permits

Detailed drawings, materials and planning applications, coordinated with engineers from the very start.

04

6 to 14 months

Construction

We are on site every week with the builder, checking details, samples and the budget, so nothing is decided by accident.

05

2 to 4 weeks

Handover

Snagging, styling and a final walk through together. We come back after a year to see how the place is being lived in.
