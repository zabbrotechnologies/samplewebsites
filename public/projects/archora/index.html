01 / ARCHITECTURAL VISION CASE STUDY MONOGRAPH

Spaces That Belong.

A digital architectural monograph rooted in Mediterranean landscape, monolithic concrete, and tactile stillness.

The ARCHORA studio experience is engineered to evoke the sensory atmosphere of a high-end architectural monograph. Rather than behaving like a hurried commercial website, it invites slow, contemplative reading through quiet negative space, deliberate serif typography, and physical materiality.

Every detail—from the gentle sunlight rays filtering through warm porcelain rooms to the weight of board-formed concrete walls—embodies the studio's tenet: architecture should look as if it grew from the stones beneath it.

02 / EDITORIAL TIMELINE DESIGN METHODOLOGY

From Topography to Permanence.

01

Topography & Sun

Three-dimensional LiDAR survey and solar azimuth mapping across seasonal solstices to locate natural shelter.

02

Material Archetypes

Sourcing indigenous stone quarries, volcanic pozzolana, and raw lime binders within the building's regional radius.

03

Carving Void

Subtracting physical mass to sculpt air, shadow, and sightlines. Interior courtyards and reflecting pools take shape.

04

Generational Weight

Engineering unyielding structural detailing intended to endure centuries without synthetic coatings or artificial veneers.

03 / SPATIAL EMBODIMENT TACTILE PALETTE

Honest Materiality.

The spatial language is defined by five raw geological and artisanal substances left untreated to celebrate natural aging.

MAT • 01

Cast Concrete

Rough timber grain impressions providing thermal inertia and organic relief.

MAT • 02

Travertine Stone

Open-vein honed Roman travertine capturing warm amber sunset reflections.

MAT • 03

Mineral Plaster

Hand-troweled lime and volcanic ash plaster with micro-tactile surface sheen.

MAT • 04

Smoked Oak

Fumed European white oak finished with cold-pressed natural linseed oils.

MAT • 05

Fluted Glass

Low-iron textured glazing that refracts direct sunlight into diffused luminescence.
