(PROCESS)

how DO we work

how DO we work

01

Discovery & Listening

We sit (virtually or in-person) for 90 minutes. You talk. We sketch. We leave with your wishlist, non-negotiables, and one thing that surprised you about your own space.

02

Concept & Spatial Strategy

We deliver two distinct design directions. Each includes floor plans, material palettes, lighting concepts, and a rough budget. You choose the soul of your project.

03

Development & Documentation

Every drawer, every outlet, every finish. We produce construction-ready drawings, millwork elevations, and a binded specification book. Your contractor will thank us.

04

Project Management

We order, track, store, and stage everything. You don't answer delivery calls or sign for pallets. Our vendors know us. Your weekends stay yours.

05

Installation & The Reveal

Our team installs for 3–5 days. Art hangs. Sofas land. Linens fold. Then we hand you the keys, light the first candle, and disappear. The rest is your story.

(FAQ)

question and answer

question and answer

(01)
How does your design process start?

We begin with a 90-minute discovery call (complimentary) to understand your space, lifestyle, and visual references. Then we create a concept mood board and rough budget estimate before any contract.

(02)
Do you work remotely or only on-site?

Both. We have teams in NYC, LA, and Chicago, but we’ve completed full projects in Miami, Aspen, and even Paris using our remote material-sampling and 3D walkthrough system.

(03)
What is your typical project timeline?

A single room: 6–10 weeks. A full home (2000–4000 sq ft): 4–7 months from concept to installation. Larger commercial spaces vary.

(04)
Can I use my existing furniture?

Absolutely. We love meaningful pieces. We’ll design around your heritage items, reupholster, or reposition them to fit the new language.

(05)
Do you offer e-design / online-only packages?

Yes – our “Digital Blueprint” package includes a full floor plan, material board, shopping list, and 2 video calls. Starting at $1,800.

(06)
How do you handle budget overruns?

We guarantee not to exceed the final approved estimate by more than 10% without your written sign-off. Transparency is a core value.
