Pinus sylvestris NT-P01

Scots Pine (Furu)

Dense resin-rich heartwood harvested from high latitudes. Unmatched natural resistance to wood-rot fungi, used historically for stave churches and ships.

06 / STUDIO MANIFESTO

"Wood remembers the sun, the wind, and the snow that shaped its grain."

NORDIC TIMBER® is an independent architectural research studio founded in Trondheim. We bridge centuries-old Scandinavian timber joinery techniques with modern structural computational modeling.

Our team of vernacular architectural historians, structural engineers, and parametric designers documents threatened timber monuments and guides zero-carbon modern mass-timber construction throughout the Nordic region.

142 Heritage Structures Surveyed
38 Vector Joinery Blueprints

Dr. Astrid Lindqvist Principal Researcher, Trondheim Field Station
