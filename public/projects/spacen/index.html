NASA // HISTORICAL 1969

Apollo 11 Lunar Landing

The first crewed mission to touch down on the lunar surface at the Sea of Tranquility, returning foundational geological core samples to Earth.

TARGET: MOON CREW: 3 MISSION SUCCESS
NASA / ESA / JAXA / CSA 1998 – PRESENT

International Space Station

Over two decades of continuous human presence conducting microgravity biotechnology, fluid dynamics, and long-duration physiological experiments.

ORBIT: LEO (400 KM) CREW: 7 OPERATIONAL
NASA / ESA / CSA 2021 – PRESENT

James Webb Space Telescope

Premier infrared space observatory stationed at Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 2, examining the earliest stars, nebular chemistry, and exoplanet atmospheres.

ORBIT: SUN-EARTH L2 MIRROR: 6.5M SCIENCE MODE
NASA / JPL 2020 – PRESENT

Perseverance Rover & Ingenuity

Seeking signs of ancient microbial life in Jezero Crater and caching geological core samples for prospective Mars Sample Return missions.

TARGET: MARS PAYLOAD: 1,025 KG ACTIVE TRAVERSE
NASA / GLOBAL PARTNERS 2022 – PRESENT

Artemis Lunar Architecture

Establishing a sustainable, long-term human presence on the Moon's South Pole to test technologies for human missions to Mars.

TARGET: LUNAR SOUTH POLE CREW: 4 DEPLOYMENT PHASE
